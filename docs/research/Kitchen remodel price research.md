# Price quartz high, compare Home Depot narrowly

Installed quartz in Northern Virginia runs about **$60–$120/sq ft** in October 2026. Entry or promo quartz bottoms out near **$74–$84/sq ft** all-in, mid-range brands (Silestone, MSI) sell for **$70–$90**, and premium brands (Caesarstone, Cambria) for **$100–$120**. A new Section 201 quartz tariff (25% in-quota, 50% over-quota, effective Aug 15, 2026) means most published local prices, which predate it, probably understate what imported quartz costs now. The Home Depot small-parts basis is now mostly verified. A sink, dishwasher and disposal hookup kit, without the fixtures themselves, costs about **$124**, and a jobsite consumables and protection basket about **$268**, both before 6% tax. Pricing the same 12-box 10x10 layout in Hampton Bay Shaker Satin White, with fillers and trim, comes to **$4,033.55 before tax** (about $4,276 with 6% tax) on homedepot.com as of Oct 23, 2026. That figure is materials only. Home Depot publishes no US dollar price for cabinet installation, countertop fabrication or a 10x10 package, so the only honest customer-facing comparison is a materials-to-materials one, dated and caveated. An "installed at Home Depot" number cannot be sourced without getting a real HD quote for a NoVA ZIP code.

### The Aug 15 tariff makes spring 2026 promos stale

The safeguard proclamation, signed Jul 31, 2026, imposes a **four-year tariff-rate quota** on quartz surface products entered on or after **Aug 15, 2026**. Imports from Canada, Mexico, Korea, CAFTA-DR, Colombia, Australia and Israel are excluded, as are imports from small-share developing countries ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/08/united-states-quartz-surface-products-safeguard-trq.html)). A retailer summary gives the year-1 terms. The quota is **140M sq ft**, in-quota duty is **25%** (falling to 23/21/19% in later years), over-quota duty is **50%** (falling to 49/48/47%), and the duty stacks on existing AD/CVD. It describes the impact as a "25% to 50%" landed markup on slabs ([GoSource](https://www.gosource.us/blog/the-2026-quartz-tariff-quota)). Regional fabricators predicted install-price increases of "at least 25%" and "30%–50%" ([Axios Pittsburgh](https://www.axios.com/local/pittsburgh/2026/01/22/quartz-tariffs-countertop-price-hike)).

My own estimate is that the hit to installed price is smaller than the hit to slab cost, because labor is not tariffed. If slab is 40–60% of the installed price, a 25% duty implies roughly **+10–15% installed**. That is arithmetic, not a measured local change. Domestic Cambria and Korean-origin slabs (LX Viatera, HanStone) avoid the safeguard. That makes them the tiers whose pre-August quotes are still most trustworthy. The Richstone and AQG prices date from April and July 2026, so they predate the tariff. Re-quote imported colors before honoring them.

## What a "vs Home Depot" page can and cannot say

| Defensible claim (with date + source on page) | Not defensible from this research |
|---|---|
| "A comparable Hampton Bay Shaker Satin White cabinet and trim package for this layout listed at **$4,033.55** on homedepot.com on Oct 23, 2026, before tax, delivery and installation." | Any "Home Depot installed" total for cabinets, countertops or the whole kitchen |
| Line-by-line HD SKU prices with links | "We're X% cheaper than Home Depot" (no sourced HD project quote exists) |
| "Home Depot charges a $129 in-home measure (credited) and quotes installation after measuring." | Using HD Canada CAD quartz prices as US prices |
| "Home Depot's cost guide lists a national average minor kitchen remodel of $24,000" ([HD](https://homedepot.com/services/c/cost-kitchen-remodel/f8b9df102)), labeled as a national average for a 200 sq ft kitchen | Presenting the $24,000 average as HD's price for *this* kitchen |
| "Our quote includes [X], which HD lists as quoted separately" (e.g., HD notes plumbing and electrical rewiring are not typically in initial quotes) ([HD](https://homedepot.com/services/c/cost-kitchen-remodel/f8b9df102)) | Quality claims such as "HD boxes are particleboard" (not verified here) or "same quality as HD" |
| Spec differences: 91.5" vs 96" trim, 84" fridge panel, skin vs decorative panel, no 21" 3-drawer in the line | Treating HD new-card "$25/$50 off" offers, or its up-to-30%-off Thomasville/KraftMaid/American Woodmark promos, as Hampton Bay prices |

The structure that survives scrutiny is a materials-to-materials table, plus a line that says "Home Depot installation: quoted after a $129 measure; not published." If you want an installed comparison, the only clean route is an actual HD quote for one NoVA ZIP code. Comparative advertising that names a competitor's prices should be dated and easy to reproduce. That is general prudence, not a researched legal finding. Note too that **prices fluctuate**. The Karran sink was already on sale, and the Hampton Bay pricing shows signs of markdowns, so refresh the snapshot before each publication.

## Conclusion

The pricing edge sits in the countertop, not the cabinet boxes. A 35 sq ft quartz job spans roughly $2,600 to $5,000+ by tier, and post-August tariff pass-through on imported slabs could add a further ~10–15% installed. That means re-quoting stale imported-color prices from the spring matters more than shaving cents off hookup parts, which total only about $560 per kitchen across the verified plumbing, electrical and consumables baskets. On the Home Depot comparison, $4,033.55 is a real and reproducible number, but only for boxes and trim. The strongest honest pitch is therefore transparency, not a percentage. Show the customer HD's dated list price for the same box sizes, state plainly what HD does not publish (install, countertop, delivery), and itemize everything your quote includes. One $129 HD measure in a NoVA ZIP would turn the comparison from materials-only into a true installed-to-installed figure, and the spend is likely worth it.
